Shade Twin: your shade, in any brand

ProblemEvery brand names shades differently, so buying base online is a gamble.
What I didMatch your shade from a brand you own or a selfie.
GoalFewer wrong-shade returns, more new brands tried.
roleUX/UI Designer
typeSelf-initiated
toolsFigma, Claude
try it
Find your perfect foundation match›
Select Shade

Your photo never leaves your browser. It’s read once to sample your skin tone.

Concept demo. Brand names are for illustration only; shade names and matches are simulated, not real brand data. Not affiliated with Nykaa or these brands.

Read the full case study

Who it’s for

  • The brand switcher, whose shade is out of stock or who wants to try something new
  • The first-time buyer who doesn’t know her shade
  • The gifter, who only knows what her friend uses

Key features

  1. Match from a brand you already use, with a confidence label
  2. Match from a guided selfie, then try it on with Virtual Try-On
  3. A saved Shade Profile that marks your shade on every product
  4. “Not sure? Get two” suggests minis of both close shades

Design decisions

  • Show confidence honestly: exact, close, or try both
  • Explain the match in plain words, like “slightly warmer”
  • Guide the selfie with live lighting tips
  • Privacy first, with the photo deleted right after matching

Builds on what exists

Nykaa already offers Virtual Try-On and Skin Scan. Shade Twin connects to them rather than replacing them.

Success metrics

  • Add-to-cart rate on base makeup
  • Returns due to wrong shade
  • Shoppers trying a new brand after a match

Concept demo with sample products. Not affiliated with Nykaa.

Read the full case study

Who it’s for

  • The value seeker who wants the best deal
  • The gift collector chasing a free sample
  • The quick buyer who wants to pay without fine print

Key features

  1. Offers bar at the top with one-tap apply
  2. Grouped sections: on offer, full price, gifts and samples
  3. Gift progress bar with smart add-on suggestions
  4. A clear savings summary and sticky pay bar

Design decisions

  • Savings before cost, so the last step feels good
  • Group items by meaning, not by when they were added
  • No surprises: fees and conditions shown in the cart
  • Gifts feel earned, with a small unlock moment

Connects to my work

Builds on the payment pages I designed at Hungama and my Payment System UI challenge.

Success metrics

  • Cart abandonment rate
  • Offer application rate
  • Average order value
  • Pricing-related support queries

Pink Pass: a sale that knows what you want

ProblemSales bury what you actually want under hundreds of offers.
What I didA personal deals feed, target price alerts, and a savings meter.
GoalMore wishlist conversions during sales.
roleUX/UI Designer
typeSelf-initiated
focusMotion and delight
try it
Sale opens in00:05
Deals for you
saved this sale₹0

Concept demo with sample products. Not affiliated with Nykaa.

Read the full case study

Who it’s for

  • The planner who builds a wishlist weeks ahead
  • The deal hunter who gets lost in endless scrolling
  • The restocker who just wants her regulars, fast

Key features

  1. A “for you” deals feed, led by wishlist and past buys
  2. Target prices with alerts when the sale hits them
  3. A “your regulars” row for one-tap restocking
  4. Timed deal drops and an animated savings meter

Design decisions

  • Personal before promotional
  • Usual price, sale price, and saving shown together
  • Delight in small doses, at moments that matter
  • Alerts only for items you chose to track

Connects to my work

Builds directly on the festive campaign UI and micro-interactions I designed at Hungama.

Success metrics

  • Wishlist conversion during sales
  • Clicks on personal deals vs generic banners
  • Price alert opt-in rate
